# Data availability and academic access

## Public datasets

FER-2013 and JAFFE are available from their original distributors under their
respective terms of use. Obtain these datasets independently; no dataset is
included in this repository. Public availability does not transfer copyright or
remove the original usage conditions.

- FER-2013: https://www.kaggle.com/c/challenges-in-representation-learning-facial-expression-recognition-challenge/data
- JAFFE: https://zenodo.org/records/3451524

## FER-Students

FER-Students is not publicly available due to privacy restrictions. Researchers
may request access for academic research by contacting
**wangjiayu4353@stu.sau.edu.cn**, providing their institutional affiliation, a
description of the proposed research, and supporting documentation confirming
its academic purpose.

Requests will be reviewed individually, and any access granted will be subject
to applicable permissions, privacy requirements, and restrictions on
redistribution. Do not send private face images, identifiable manifests, or
supporting identity documents through public GitHub issues.

The exact materials that may be provided and their usage conditions will be
confirmed during review. An academic-purpose statement does not automatically
entitle a requester to receive the images. Approval of access must precede data
transfer; the code license does not license dataset content.
